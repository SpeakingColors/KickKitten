# Intro
Kick Kitten makes trance kicks. Three layers (click, body, sub) follow one pitch envelope; a colour stage (Purr Trio), a Sauce stage, glue, tone and a limiter finish the sound. It is a VST3 instrument and a standalone app. Play a note and you get the kick; the kick is rendered in the background, so turning a knob updates the sound within a moment.

Kick Kitten is free and open source (AGPL-3.0-or-later). It has no telemetry, makes no network calls and collects nothing.

This is a test build. The sound engine was checked by numbers against the mockup, but the plugin has not been tuned by ear yet.

# Quick start
- Pick a preset with the arrows in the header, or click the preset name for the list.
- Press Hit (or the Space bar) to hear the kick. Loop plays a kick on every fourth 16th note at the BPM you set; Bass test adds a simple bass one octave above the tail note.
- Set Tail to the root note of your track, so kick and bass add up instead of fighting.
- Shape the pitch with the pitch envelope: drag a dot, drag the line to bend it, double click to add or remove a dot.
- In your DAW, play any MIDI note. Same plays the same kick on every key; Pitched re-renders the kick for each note.
- Load a kick you like with Reference  to see its spectrum in amber, then press Match to move Decay, Shape, Hold, Swell, Tone EQ, Thump, Punch, Crack and the click settings toward it. Start from the sound you have; load the preset again to undo.
- The Render tab renders the kick at a chosen key and velocity; save it with Export .wav or drag its waveform into your DAW.
- Drag the waveform into your DAW to drop the kick as a .wav, or use Export for a 24-bit mono .wav.

# Recommendations
- Tune Tail to your track's root note first, then adjust Start and Sweep. About 25 to 35 ms of Sweep is tight.
- Use the click to keep the kick audible inside a wall of supersaws; raise it a little rather than raising the whole kick.
- Check the 16th readout in Output and timing: it shows how long the tail runs against one 16th at your BPM.
- Watch the meter. Ceiling sets the true-peak limit of the finished kick.
- Save your own presets with Save; they are plain files you can copy and share.

# Install
- The installer for your system is in the zip, with an uninstaller. Everything works offline.
- Your saved presets are kept when you uninstall unless you choose to delete them.
